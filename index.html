Level 1: The Basics

1. What is Matter?

Matter is the physical "stuff" of the universe. Anything that has **Mass** (physical material) and **Volume** (takes up 3D space) is matter.

Mass vs. Weight

Mass never changes! A 60 kg astronaut on Earth has a mass of 60 kg on the Moon. However, their weight (gravity pull) is 1/6th on the Moon.

Measuring Volume
  • Regular Solids: $L \times W \times H$
  • Liquids: Graduated cylinders
  • Irregular Solids: Water Displacement Method
What is NOT matter? Pure energy (sunlight, heat, sound) and abstract things (thoughts, shadows).

Level 2: Deepening Understanding

1. Thermal Expansion & Contraction

Added heat creates kinetic energy, pushing particles further apart (**Expansion**). Cooling removes energy (**Contraction**).

Global Engineering: Expansion loops along the Trans-Ecuadorian oil pipeline absorb steel growth during hot afternoons!

Level 3: Expert Analysis

1. Heating Curves & Latent Heat

$\text{Total Heat} = \text{KE Increase (Temp Change)} + \text{PE Increase (Phase Change)}$. During plateaus, temperature stays constant because energy breaks bonds (**Latent Heat**).

2. Brownian Motion & Diffusion

Brownian Motion: Erratic random motion caused by collisions with fast, invisible fluid particles.
Diffusion: Particles spreading from High to Low concentration. Faster in gases and at High Temps!

3. Anomalous Water Expansion

The Marine Lifeline

Water reaches max density at 4°C. As it cools to 0°C, it forms an open hexagonal lattice, becoming less dense and floating as ice. This insulates deep Siberian lakes, protecting fish below!
